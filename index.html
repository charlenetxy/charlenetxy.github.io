About

I am Charlene, a final-year student at NTU majoring in Mathematical Sciences (Statistics) with a second major in Data Analytics, graduating in July 2027. My coursework has covered a range of topics in statistics and data analysis, including statistical inference and predictive modelling. Throughout my studies, I realised that I enjoy turning data into practical answers to real problems.

I started out liking maths and problem solving, and data analysis feels like a natural extension of that, where I can find insights in datasets far too large for anyone to read through, and use them to help people make decisions. The projects I have enjoyed most involve real problems where the findings point to something that could be improved, and my favourite part is working out what the results mean and applying them back to the problem.

I am careful and methodical in how I work, and open to learning new tools and techniques. I am looking for internships and entry-level roles, preferably in Singapore or remote, but open to other locations.
